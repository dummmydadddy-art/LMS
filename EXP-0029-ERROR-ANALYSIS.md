## 1. Executive Forensic Summary

The forensic evaluation of EXP-0029 examines the transition from **B5** (EXP-0028 Champion) to **C7** (Full Pre-Filter). The analysis focuses on three core mechanisms:
1. **OOD Parametric Leakage Elimination:** Entity-grounding successfully blocks external framework inquiries (e.g. Svelte, MongoDB, Go, Vite, Rollup) from triggering spurious Pass-1 entailments.
2. **Ambiguity Gate Effectiveness:** Subjective adjectives ('best', 'optimal', 'preferable') and open-ended design inquiries are gated to `ABSTAIN` or `BLOCK`, preventing Pass-2 over-refutation.
3. **Residual Failure Taxonomy:** Surviving failures in C7 stem primarily from nuanced multi-sentence assertions and subtle lexical overlap where external tools share standard terminology.

## 4. Failure Mode Taxonomy in C7

Across the remaining failures in C7, the error distribution is:
1. **Subtle Antonym/Polarity Misses:** Complex sentence structures where neither Tier 1 regex nor Tier 2 Pass 1 crosses the 0.70 confidence threshold.
2. **Complex Multi-Clause Claims:** Claims with two distinct technical clauses where evidence only matches one clause, causing partial grounding ambiguity.
3. **Adversarial Prefix Distraction:** A small subset of prompt-injection queries where strong adversarial framing obscures the core proposition from Tier 1 proposition extraction.
